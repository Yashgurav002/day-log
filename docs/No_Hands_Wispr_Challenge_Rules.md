# No Hands: The Wispr Challenge — Complete Rules & Reference

**Organisers:** WeMakeDevs × Wispr Flow
**Source:** https://www.wemakedevs.org/wispr (+ /judges, /schedule, /ideas)
**Captured:** 22 October 2026
**Format:** Online, solo, 16 – 30 October 2026

> Purpose of this document: a complete reference of the challenge rules, to be used together with a project idea when planning and building a submission. Every project decision should be checked against Section 12 (Compliance Checklist).

---

## 1. What the challenge is

- A two-week online hackathon from WeMakeDevs and Wispr Flow, **16 to 30 October**.
- Goal: **fix a real problem in your own work by building a tool for it.**
- **The twist: you must use your voice (Wispr Flow) for the building process** — prompts, specs, instructions, docs, messages and commit notes are spoken, not typed.
- Tagline: *"Build it, write it, ship it. Just don't type it."*

### The intended shape of a project
1. **The problem** — start with whatever slows you down in your own work. Examples from the site: the report you rebuild from the same spreadsheets every Monday; the WhatsApp questions you answer with the same three replies; the CRM nobody updates because the write-up takes longer than the call.
2. **The build** — make a tool that fixes it. Tell your AI tools what you want the way you'd tell a colleague; Wispr Flow turns speech into clean text in whatever app you're using.
3. **The people** — you don't need to be a developer (see Tracks).

---

## 2. What Wispr Flow is

- Hold a key and talk; polished text lands wherever your cursor is, in any app.
- Claimed ~4× faster than typing.
- Participants get **1 month of Wispr Flow Pro free** via the challenge link (promo code `WEMAKEDEVS`).

---

## 3. Eligibility

- **Anyone in India.** Professionals across all domains (founders, developers, marketing, product, HR, etc.) and **students are welcome**.
- **Solo only — no teams.** Every entry is one person's project.
- **Late joining allowed:** registration stays open until submissions close (30 Oct, 11:59 pm IST).
- **Requirements:** a Mac or Windows computer with a microphone, and a Wispr Flow account.
- **Cost:** free to enter.

---

## 4. How it works (4 steps)

1. **Register** — Set up Wispr Flow *before* registering. **Make your first Flow the start of your project:** say the problem in your work you want to fix and what your tool should do.
2. **Build with Flow** — Pick a track and talk your way through it: the spec to your AI tools, the docs, the messages. *Keyboard for shortcuts, voice for the rest.*
3. **Share every week** — Upload your Wispr usage card and post your progress. Weekly winners every Monday.
4. **Submit your project** — On the website by **30 October, 11:59 pm IST**.

**Why Wispr must be set up before registering:** the challenge is about solving problems voice-first; prompts, specs and docs are spoken, so Flow must be working before you start building.

---

## 5. Tracks

**One rule for both tracks: use your voice.** Either way you ship **a working tool, built by voice, and one real result it produced.**
**You may enter only ONE track and submit only ONE project.**

### Track 1 — Builders (for developers)
- **What:** A working software product that solves a problem in your own work.
- **How:** Dictate requirements and follow-ups into your coding tools and let them do the coding.
- **For:** Full-stack developers, AI engineers, mobile developers, DevOps engineers.
- **Suggested tools to talk into:** Cursor, Claude Code, v0, ChatGPT.

### Track 2 — No-Code (for every other role)
- **What:** A working tool for your own job, made with an AI app builder or assistant, no coding needed.
- **For:** Marketers, product managers, sales, founders, designers, HR, ops.
- **What counts:** A tool someone else can open and use:
  - an app made with an AI app builder (e.g. Lovable), or
  - a reusable assistant shared by link (custom GPT, Gemini Gem), or
  - a workflow in Zapier, Make or Notion that runs when the judges trigger it (e.g. from a form).
- **What does NOT count:** a chat thread or a one-off prompt.
- **Access rule:** Judges must be able to try it **without paying or asking you for access**. Share a public link; if sign-in is needed, include a **test login and sample input** in your description.

### Bonus categories (ranked separately)
- **Most Wispr usage** — Upload your Wispr usage card each week. Ranked by **words dictated during the challenge, in any app**.
- **Social / build in public** — Post progress with **#NoHandsChallenge**, tag **WeMakeDevs** and **Wispr Flow**, and add the post link to your weekly check-in. Ranked by how clearly a post shows a real workflow.

## 6. Voice-usage rules

- Use your voice **for as much as you can**: prompts, docs, messages and commit notes.
- Keyboard is allowed for **shortcuts and small fixes**.
- **Judges look at how much of your writing went through Flow.**
- Usage is measured from the **usage card in the Wispr Flow desktop app → Insights → Your usage**.
- Only words dictated **during the challenge (16 – 30 Oct)** count for the usage ranking.
- Winners' numbers / Wispr accounts are **verified with Wispr** before prizes are sent.

---

## 7. Tools

- **Any editor, AI assistant or app builder is allowed.** Wispr Flow is how you talk to them.
- The free month covers **Wispr Flow Pro only**. Lovable, Cursor, ChatGPT, Claude, etc. need your own accounts.
- Their **free plans are enough**, but they cap messages/credits per day — **start early**.
- **You don't have to pay for anything to take part.**

## 9. Judging

Four questions, weighted, **fixed before the challenge starts**:

1. **Does it solve a real problem in your work?** — A specific problem, a clear user, and a tool that person would keep using.
2. **Was Wispr used meaningfully?** — Flow is part of the build *and* the workflow, and your demo and usage card show it.
3. **Does it work?** — A live link the judges can open and use.
4. **Is the demo clear?** — A two-minute video anyone can follow.

> Note: the percentage weights are loaded dynamically on the site and were not visible at capture time (shown as "0%"). Check the live /wispr/judges page.

### Fairness rules
1. Criteria and weights are fixed before the challenge starts.
2. Every project is scored by **multiple judges**; final score is the **average**.
3. Every judge's score for every project is **public** on the leaderboard.
4. Every project gets **written comments** on how it could improve.
5. Judges do not score a project they have a connection to.
6. Scores are published **Wednesday 4 November**; questions can be raised until end of **Thursday 5 November**, with a written reply before winners are confirmed.
7. Winners' Wispr accounts are verified with Wispr before prizes are sent.

## 14. Things the rules do NOT say (grey areas)

- **Existing / pre-built projects:** not explicitly banned or allowed. However, the first Flow is supposed to *start* the project, judges score how much writing went through Flow, and usage only counts 16 – 30 Oct. **Safe approach:** if building on earlier code, make a substantial voice-driven upgrade inside the window and state clearly in the description what existed before and what was added.
- **Specific tech stack / hosting:** not restricted; must be live and free for judges to use.
- **Open-source repo:** not required (unlike many hackathons) — only a working link.

## 15. Compliance checklist (use with any project idea)

**Idea fit**
- [ ] Solves a **specific, real problem from my own work** (not a generic/civic/hypothetical problem).
- [ ] Has a **clear user** (me / my role) who would **keep using it**.
- [ ] Can produce **one real result** on real (or realistic) data to show in the demo.
- [ ] Fits **exactly one track** (Builders if I write code via AI tools; No-Code otherwise).
- [ ] Buildable solo before **30 Oct, 11:59 pm IST**, using only **free tiers**.

**Process**
- [ ] Wispr Flow installed and working **before** registering.
- [ ] **First Flow** = describing the problem and what the tool should do.
- [ ] Prompts, specs, docs, messages and **commit notes dictated** via Flow; keyboard only for shortcuts/small fixes.
- [ ] Weekly check-in with usage card screenshot (by Mon 26 Oct and Mon 2 Nov).
- [ ] (Bonus) Progress posts with #NoHandsChallenge tagging WeMakeDevs and Wispr Flow.

**Deliverables**
- [ ] **Live link**, opens without payment or access requests; test login + sample input provided if sign-in exists; sample data visible on first screen.
- [ ] **Demo video ≤ 2:00**, clear to anyone, visibly shows Flow being used and the real result.
- [ ] **Wispr usage card** screenshot (Insights → Your usage).
- [ ] **Description:** problem, who it's for, how Wispr was used (+ what was pre-existing, if anything).
- [ ] No confidential/personal data in the public demo.
